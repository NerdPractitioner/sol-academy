Thanks for playing!

Could you answer 5 quick questions about that fight? It takes about 2 minutes, and honest answers help most, including "I was bored" or "I had no idea what was happening".

Give feedback

Some numbers about how you played (fights, wins, which moves you used) are attached to the form. Nothing personal. You're welcome to keep fighting too.

Wind-ups

Every move takes ticks to land. The track at the top shows who fires when, and how strong each beam is.

Chakra

Beams, Power Charge and Assist cost chakra. Strikes give 3 back, and each turn regains 2.

Beam timing

Choose how long to charge. Arc Beam 2–4t, Solar Lance 3–6t, Nova Cannon 4–8t. Longer means stronger and costlier.

⚡ Beams

Only ⚡ moves are beams: Arc Beam, Solar Lance, Nova Cannon and the Warden's Gravity Beam. Beams draw solid lines; melee draws dashes. Only a beam can clash with a beam.

Beam Clash

Meet an enemy beam with your own. More power starts you ahead. Losing still soaks 20–70% of the hit.

Team up

Assist takes 1 tick, adds +16 and infuses your element: Kai burns, Rook shells the charger, Vex shocks (delays the target 2t). Mixing elements adds combo power.

Grapple

Rook runs in (1t) and pins the target of an ally's charge. The pinned enemy is frozen and takes +25%. Rook eats 20% splash; a 20+ hit breaks his grip.

React

Reactions are never free. An idle hero loses time (Dive in +2t, Brace +1t). A charging hero loses power instead (−30% / −15%).

Focus & bailing out

Hits on a charging hero can shake focus (damage × 2.5% chance): −25% power and +1t. About to be hit mid-charge? Fire early at 80%, or abort and get half your chakra back.

Keys 1–9 pick menu options. Resolve fills when you take hits and land big attacks.
